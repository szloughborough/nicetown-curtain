Choosing NICETOWN curtain rods

Start with the window: measure the rod width and the drop you want, check whether the set includes one or two panels, then pick the fabric and construction that suit the room. Every product on this page states the size and panel count we have confirmed. The current price, available colors, and final options are on Amazon.

Questions about NICETOWN curtain rods

Rods and accessories are listed as separate items in our catalog. Curtain product names state the panel count and heading style, which tells you what kind of rod the panel needs. Check both listings before ordering if you are putting together a full setup.
Valances and kitchen tiers are often single panels, unlike our standard two-panel window sets. The panel count appears in each product name. Check the stated width and length against your window before ordering.
A tieback holds a curtain open to one side so more daylight comes through the window. Our catalog lists tiebacks as accessories, and some sidelight and door curtains include one. Check the product name for included details.
Compare the color name, fabric, and heading style in both product titles rather than the photo alone. Sizes also differ between window panels and short tiers or valances. Each product page lists the details we have confirmed for that item.
Short tier and valance styles in our catalog are sized for smaller kitchen windows, with width and length stated in the product name. Measure the window and the rod position before choosing. These are usually single panels rather than pairs.
