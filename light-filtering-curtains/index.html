Questions about NICETOWN light filtering curtains

Light filtering panels let daylight through while softening it and adding daytime privacy. They are not designed to darken a room. Our range includes semi-sheer weaves, non-see-through voile, and linen-textured fabrics, with the fabric named in each product title.
Non-see-through styles are named that way in our catalog, but any light fabric will behave differently with interior lights on after dark. If evening privacy is the priority, consider pairing a sheer with a blackout panel on a double rod, or choose a room-darkening style instead.
You can, particularly in a room where you want soft morning light. For sleep spaces that need less light, our blackout and 100% blackout panels are the better match. Compare the fabric and liner details in each product name before choosing.
Most standard window styles are two-panel sets, and the panel count is stated in the product name. The width given covers both panels. Check it before ordering so the fabric has enough room to gather when the curtains are closed.
The collection includes voile, semitransparent linen-textured weaves, and polyester blends. Fabric is named in the product title where we have it confirmed. If you are matching an existing set, compare the color name and heading style as well as the fabric.
