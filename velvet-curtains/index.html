NICETOWN Velvet Curtains

NICETOWN velvet curtains give a room a heavier drape and a deeper color than a standard polyester panel. Our velvet range covers blackout and room-darkening constructions in black, brown, navy, and seasonal tones, with 84 and 96 inch lengths and grommet or rod pocket headings. Two-panel sets suit most standard windows; wider patio panels are listed separately for sliding doors and media rooms. Some styles combine the velvet face with thermal or blackout backing. We publish the size, color, and heading style verified for each product so you can compare the exact configuration before buying on Amazon.
