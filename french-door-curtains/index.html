NICETOWN Blackout French Door Curtains

NICETOWN French door curtains are cut for door glass, sidelights, and wide sliding openings rather than a standard window. Our range includes single wide patio panels, narrow sidelight curtains with tiebacks, and blackout room divider drapes for doorways. Common lengths are 84 and 95 inches, with grommet, rod pocket, and tie-up headings. Most door panels are sold individually, so read the panel count before comparing them with our two-panel window sets. Thermal and blackout versions also help with light and temperature near an exterior door. We publish the verified size, panel count, and heading style for each product on Amazon.
