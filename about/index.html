Our NICETOWN curtain story

NICETOWN started in 1999 as a curtain-focused workshop. Over the following decades we grew into a home decor brand with a wider range that includes bedding, rugs, and accessories, while curtains stayed at the center of what we make.

Our design approach brings together European and American aesthetics with precision manufacturing. We invest in automated production and quality-control systems, and our materials go through testing before they reach a finished panel.

That history shows up in the catalog on this site: blackout and 100% blackout liners, thermal insulated constructions, sheer and voile weaves, linen and velvet textures, and panels cut specifically for French doors, sidelights, and patios.

What NICETOWN curtains cover

Blackout and 100% blackout panels with liners, thermal insulated drapes, sheer and voile weaves, linen and velvet textures, printed and seasonal designs, plus panels cut for French doors, sidelights, sliding doors, and outdoor openings.
