Privacy Policy

This policy describes how nicetown-curtain.com handles information when you visit our product showcase. Last updated November 15, 2026.

Who operates this site

This site is operated by Nicetown for United States visitors. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@nicetown-curtain.com.

Information you give us

If you email us, we receive the address and the content of your message so we can respond. This site has no account system, shopping cart, or checkout, and we do not ask you to submit an order or payment here.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested in server logs. We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used. These services use cookies and similar technologies to measure visits, page views, and interactions. Their measurement data is processed by Google and Microsoft under their own terms.

Links to Amazon and other sites

Our product buttons link to Amazon.com, where purchases are completed. Some of these links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you. Once you leave this site, Amazon's privacy practices apply. Amazon may also measure the referral that brought you there.

How we use information

We use the information described above to answer messages, keep the site working, understand which collections visitors find useful, and measure referrals to our products. We do not sell personal information, and we do not build advertising profiles from it on this site.

Retention and security

We keep email correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request. Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings of the providers above. We use reasonable measures to protect the site, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is a general product showcase and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and use the analytics opt-out tools offered by Google and Microsoft. Blocking cookies may change how some measurement works, but the product pages remain readable.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date above shows the current version. For questions, email contact@nicetown-curtain.com.

This policy is an operational description of this website, not legal advice.
