NICETOWN Soundproof Curtains

NICETOWN soundproof and noise-reducing curtains use dense, layered fabric to soften the sound that comes through a window. They are often the same thermal insulated, blackout constructions found elsewhere in our catalog, so a single panel can address light and noise together. Look for grommet headings, two-panel sets, and longer drops that cover the full window frame with some overlap at the sides. Curtains reduce reflected and incoming sound; they do not seal a window the way a structural treatment would.
