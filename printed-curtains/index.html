NICETOWN Printed Curtains

Printed NICETOWN curtains add pattern to a room without changing the rest of the decor. This collection includes floral, buffalo plaid, striped, star, and seasonal designs, plus pom pom and ruffled valances for kitchens and kids rooms. Many printed panels are built on the same blackout or room-darkening fabric as our solid ranges, so a patterned curtain can still do a practical job. Sizes include short tiers, 63 inch panels, and standard 84 inch drops, usually as two-panel sets. We list the verified size, panel count, and design details with every product and link to Amazon.

Choosing NICETOWN printed curtains

Start with the window: measure the rod width and the drop you want, check whether the set includes one or two panels, then pick the fabric and construction that suit the room.
