Choosing NICETOWN blackout curtains

Start with the window: measure the rod width and the drop you want, check whether the set includes one or two panels, then pick the fabric and construction that suit the room. Every product on this page states the size and panel count we have confirmed. The current price, available colors, and final options are on Amazon.

Questions about NICETOWN blackout curtains

Our blackout panels use dense fabric, and several 100% blackout styles add a separate liner for fuller light control. Coverage also depends on fit: choose a width that lets the fabric overlap the window frame on each side. Check the panel count and drop listed with each product, then compare the construction details before you buy on Amazon.
Most of our window sets are two panels, while wide patio and sliding door curtains are often a single panel. The panel count is stated in every product name on this site, for example 2 Panels or 1 Panel. Read it before ordering, since the stated width refers to the full set.
Polyester panels are generally straightforward to care for, but cleaning instructions belong to the specific product you own. Follow the care label shipped with your curtains rather than a general guide, and remove any hardware or tiebacks before washing. If you need the exact guidance for a style, our team can help through the contact page.
Match the drop to where you want the panel to finish: sill, below the frame, or floor. Our catalog lists 63, 84, 95, and 96 inch lengths. For width, measure the rod and allow extra fabric so the panels can gather when closed; a two-panel set is measured across both panels.
Many of our blackout panels also use thermal insulated construction, which adds a layered barrier at the window. That can make a room feel more comfortable through hot afternoons and cold mornings. Look for Thermal Insulated in the product name, and compare the fabric and liner details listed for the exact configuration on Amazon.
