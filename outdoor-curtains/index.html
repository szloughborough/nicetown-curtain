NICETOWN Outdoor Curtains

NICETOWN outdoor curtains are made for patios, pergolas, gazebos, and porches where a panel has to handle open air. Our outdoor range includes waterproof fabrics in neutral tones such as biscotti beige, with longer 95 inch drops and wide panels for sliding door and patio openings. Grommet headings make the panels easy to slide along an outdoor rod, and several styles are sold as single wide panels rather than a pair. Outdoor panels are not a substitute for indoor blackout drapes. We publish the verified size, fabric, and panel count for each product, with purchase options on Amazon.

Choosing NICETOWN outdoor curtains

Start with the window: measure the rod width and the drop you want, check whether the set includes one or two panels, then pick the fabric and construction that suit the room. Every product on this page states the size and panel count we have confirmed. The current price, available colors, and final options are on Amazon.

Questions about NICETOWN outdoor curtains

Several styles in our outdoor range are named waterproof in the product title, including patio panels in neutral tones. Check the exact product name for the fabric claim, since not every outdoor panel carries it. Construction details are listed with each item on Amazon.
Measure the opening rather than the glass alone, and add width so the fabric can gather when drawn. Our outdoor range includes wide single panels and 95 inch drops for pergolas, gazebos, and sliding openings. Each product page lists the size and panel count we have confirmed.
Many wide patio and outdoor panels are single, unlike our standard window sets. The panel count appears in the product name. Read it before ordering so you have enough coverage across the full opening.
Grommet headings are common in our outdoor range because they slide easily along an outdoor rod. The heading style is named in the product title where confirmed. Match it to the rod you plan to use before ordering.
They are designed for open-air spaces such as patios and pergolas, so their fabric and weight differ from our indoor blackout panels. For a bedroom or living room, choose from the blackout, thermal, or sheer collections instead. Compare the construction details in each listing.
